> A complete school management system designed to simplify academic, administrative, and student-related activities through a centralized digital platform.

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Management

* **Student Profiles** — Manage student information and academic details.
* **Student Records** — Maintain organized student data in one place.
* **Academic Information** — Access relevant academic and enrollment information.
* **Student Dashboard** — Provide students with a centralized view of their school activities.

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher Management

* **Teacher Profiles** — Manage teacher information and records.
* **Teacher Dashboard** — Give teachers access to their academic responsibilities.
* **Class & Subject Management** — Organize classes, subjects, and teaching-related information.
* **Academic Management** — Manage teacher-related academic activities through the system.

### 🏫 School Management

* **Centralized School System** — Manage core school operations from a single platform.
* **Academic Management** — Organize academic information and school activities.
* **Class Management** — Manage classes and associated academic data.
* **Subject Management** — Maintain subjects and their related information.

### 📚 Academic Management

* **Course Management** — Organize and manage academic courses.
* **Class Scheduling** — Manage academic schedules and class-related information.
* **Academic Records** — Keep important academic information organized.
* **School Data Management** — Maintain centralized educational records.

### 👥 User & Role Management

* **Role-Based Access** — Provide different access levels based on user roles.
* **User Management** — Manage users and their associated information.
* **Secure Authentication** — Protect access to the school management system.
* **Personalized Dashboards** — Provide role-specific interfaces for different users.

### 📝 Administrative Management

* **Administrative Dashboard** — Centralized interface for managing school operations.
* **Record Management** — Manage important school records efficiently.
* **User Administration** — Manage different users and their access.
* **System Management** — Organize and control the application's core functionality.

### 📱 Flutter Application

* **Cross-Platform UI** — Built with Flutter for a modern application experience.
* **Responsive Interface** — Designed for a consistent experience across supported devices.
* **Clean Navigation** — Organized navigation between different modules.
* **Reusable Components** — Structured UI components for maintainability.

### ⚙️ Backend

* **PHP Backend** — Server-side functionality powered by PHP.
* **API Integration** — Flutter application communicates with backend services through APIs.
* **Data Management** — Centralized handling of application and school data.
* **Frontend–Backend Architecture** — Separate Flutter frontend and PHP backend for better maintainability.

* **PHP** — Backend

## 🏗️ Project Structure

The project is organized into separate frontend and backend components:

```text
eSchool/
├── eschool-flutter/
│   └── Flutter Application
│
├── eschool-backend/
│   └── PHP Backend
│
└── README.md
```

This separation keeps the mobile application and backend services organized and easier to maintain.
